Hearing Health Research & Development

Internship at Apple in Cupertino, CA

Project Overview

During my internship with Apple’s Acoustics Hearing Health R&D team in Cupertino, CA, I developed and prototyped hardware and signal processing algorithms for wearable devices and Hearing Health features in Apple's ecosystem. I led the bring-up and refinement of novel wireless technologies and DSP methods for acoustic sensing devices and, therefore designed and evaluated large scale user studies in addition to acoustic measurements and calibrations. My day-to-day work included cross-functional collaboration with hardware, software, user experience design, health, and user studies teams. I regularly presented my project progress and independently explored early-stage concepts to Apple’s (Acoustics) leadership.
