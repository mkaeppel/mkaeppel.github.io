Investigations on reproduction methods using irregular near-field loudspeaker arrays

Bachelor's Thesis at the Institute for Hearing Technology and Acoustics | RWTH Aachen University

Project Overview

A frequency-dependent decoding approach was derived and implemented to account for varying loudspeaker distances and near field effects. Mode-matching, regularized, and energy-preserving decoders were evaluated in terms of synthesis accuracy and spatial reproduction performance for different array configurations.

The work was later implemented at the Institute for Work and Engineering Psychology at RWTH Aachen University.

The paper is in German, but I'm happy to discuss and answer any questions you might have.
