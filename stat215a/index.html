Applied Data Science and Machine Learning

Stat 215A - UC Berkeley

These projects were completed as part of the Applied Data Science and Machine Learning course (Stat 215A) taught by Professor Bin Yu at UC Berkeley in Fall 2025. Labs 1-3 were completed independently, while Labs 4 and 5 were collaborative group projects.

PECARN Traumatic Brain Injury Analysis

Exploratory analysis of pediatric head trauma data to identify predictors of clinically-important TBI and develop decision rules for CT scanning.

Dialectometry and Linguistic Variation

Analyzing U.S. dialect patterns using dimensionality reduction (PCA, NMF, t-SNE) and clustering methods to identify regional linguistic clusters.

Parallelizing k-means Clustering

Implementing efficient similarity functions and stability-based clustering to determine optimal number of clusters in linguistic data.

MISR Cloud Detection with Deep Learning

Building CNN autoencoders and ensemble models to classify Arctic cloud pixels from NASA satellite imagery.

Modeling Brain Responses to Natural Language

Evaluating static and contextual word embeddings with voxel-wise encoding models to understand how semantic context is represented in human cortex.
