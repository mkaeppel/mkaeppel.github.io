Lab 3: Stability-Based Clustering and Parallelization

Stat 215A - UC Berkeley | Fall 2025

Project Overview

This lab investigates a principled approach to determining the optimal number of clusters in k-means clustering by measuring the stability of clustering solutions under data perturbations. Following the method introduced by Ben-Hur et al., we quantify stability through the similarity between clusterings obtained from different subsamples of the linguistic dialect dataset from Lab 2.

The core challenge is that comparing two clusterings requires measuring pairwise co-membership relationships, which naively scales as O(q²) for q data points. For large datasets with tens of thousands of observations, this becomes prohibitively expensive when repeated hundreds of times across multiple values of k.
